📦 Scrap Sellers Guide

How to Sell Scrap from Your Doorstep:

  1. Register & Sign In: Create your account with your Name, Phone Number, and Password to access the platform.
  2. Snap Scrap Photo: Use the AI Scrap Scanner to take a photo of your paper, plastic, metal, or e-waste.
  3. Instant AI Valuation: Gemini AI classifies material, estimates weight, and calculates your guaranteed payout based on real-time market rates.
  4. Add Pickup Details: Add your street address, landmark, and building/gate instructions.
  5. Doorstep Digital Weigh-In: Verified local collector arrives with a calibrated digital hanging or platform scale. Gross weight is weighed and container tare (sack/crate) is deducted transparently.
  6. 4-Digit Handover OTP: Receive your cash/UPI payout and share the 4-digit OTP to lock transaction and generate your digital green receipt!
🏪 Scrap Dealers / Buyers Guide

How to Claim & Collect Pickups:

  1. Live Geospatial Radar: Open the radar map to view available scrap pickups within a 5km to 25km radius.
  2. Filter Materials: Filter by specific scrap items (copper, brass, batteries, cardboard, PET bottles).
  3. Accept Pickup: Claim the listing to receive exact landmark, directions, and direct seller contact.
  4. Doorstep Station: Open Doorstep tab, load Order ID, enter gross weight, and choose tare container deduction.
  5. AI Scale Verification: Snap a photo of the scale readout for instant tamper-proof verification.
  6. OTP Settlement: Enter seller's 4-digit OTP to finalize transaction and update inventory.

Strict Ban on Prohibited & Hazardous Materials

Bio-medical waste, toxic chemicals, radioactive elements, explosives, firearms, ammunition, and public/municipal infrastructure items (e.g. street light copper cables, railway components, manhole covers) are strictly prohibited. Attempting to list stolen public property will lead to permanent blacklisting and mandatory police reporting.

2

Transparent Digital Weighing & Approved Tare Standards

All weigh-ins must take place in plain sight of both parties using a zero-calibrated digital hanging or platform scale. Tare deductions are restricted to verified standard containers: Gunny Sack (-0.5 kg), Plastic Crate (-1.5 kg), Metal Drum (-5.0 kg). Artificial weighting (adding water, rocks, or sand) is deemed fraud.

3

OTP Handover Protection & Settlement Policy

The 4-digit Handover OTP is the legal confirmation of scrap transfer. Sellers MUST NOT share the OTP until all scrap items have been weighed and cash or instant UPI payment has been received. Once the buyer submits the OTP, the transaction is non-reversible.

4

Dealer Punctuality & Verification Protocol

Approved collectors must carry valid government ID, wear RECYGO digital badges, and arrive within the scheduled 60-minute pickup window. Unjustified cancellations or repeated no-shows will trigger dealer account suspension.

5

Dispute Resolution & 24-Hour Redressal Window

Any dispute regarding rate differences, incorrect gross weight, or unpaid tare adjustments must be filed within 24 hours of the pickup via the Help modal or Admin hotline (+91 99999 99999) for mediation.
