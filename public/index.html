Help & Support

Common Help Topics

How do I list scrap?

Go to the Sell Scrap tab, complete your profile, upload a photo, and publish your listing.

How do I find nearby buyers?

Use the Find Scrap tab, select your radius, and review nearby pickup listings on the map.

How is payout calculated?

The platform uses the scrap category rate and the final net weight after tare deduction.
