Help & Guidance

Platform basics, rules, and the order workflow.

Basic guide

Buyers can review available orders, view status and raise a support query. Sellers can submit order details for admin verification and review their transaction history.

Platform rules

  • Provide accurate contact and order details.
  • Do not share passwords or one-time codes.
  • Never treat a screenshot as proof of payment without authorized verification.
  • Commission rates and transaction amounts must be transparent.

Seller guidelines

Submit complete order information and supporting references. Admins can verify or reject orders with a recorded reason. Commission is calculated from the order's gross amount and the configured percentage.

Buyer guidelines & order procedure

Submit a support query if order details are incorrect. The demo workflow is Pending Verification → Verified & Dispatched → Completed. Rejected or disputed orders should include a reason and be reviewed by an authorized admin.
